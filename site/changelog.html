A more personal
daily workspace.

These features are implemented in the current candidate. Final design and the owner’s full post-design test pass are pending. This page is not a release announcement or store-approval claim.

Everyday controls

  • A vertical wallpaper switch and adjacent quick settings on supported chat pages.
  • A provider-aware toolbar with current-site pause, global pause and site reset at the top.
  • Detailed adjustments stay under a collapsible section; settings explain unsupported pages and global pause.
  • General settings can hide the entire on-page dock without deleting your wallpaper.

More of your wallpaper, less distraction

  • Wallpaper fits the chat pane beside expanded or compact navigation.
  • Optional reading comfort follows conversation text with a feathered blur. Welcome screens and composers are untreated.
  • Cover/Contain, image position, opacity, blur and dark/light blending remain adjustable.
  • Each supported provider can use its own effect settings while sharing the same wallpaper source.

A simpler start

  • Choose Mist, Dusk or Paper, use an image, or skip setup.
  • See a synthetic conversation preview before opening a provider.
  • Existing installations keep their upload/effects and skip the new setup intro.
  • Reduced-motion GIF still frames, explicit playback, stronger focus styles, slider units and forced-color support.

What stays local

No extension account, cloud upload service or telemetry was added. Your original GIF and custom upload remain saved until you remove them. Displayed wallpapers can still be read by scripts on the supported page. Read the privacy details.

Before release

Provider layouts change. Final Chrome/Brave checks, full hands-on accessibility testing and upgrade/recovery verification will happen after design. Reading comfort does not guarantee text contrast over every image. The reported Gemini account-visibility issue was not reproduced in the earlier layout and remains a manual acceptance item.
