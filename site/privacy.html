Your wallpaper
stays local.

This describes the extension candidate included with this local launch page. Final design, owner review and post-design testing are pending. No store submission or public launch has taken place.

What stays in your browser

LLM Wallpapers stores your chosen image, its source filename, selected preset, visual effects, provider overrides and setup/motion preferences in local browser extension storage. It does not use a project server, an extension account, cloud sync, analytics or advertising.

Static uploads are resized and encoded locally. GIFs remain saved in their original form. A temporary decoded still frame is used when reduced motion is requested or a preview is paused. That frame is held in page memory, not uploaded; cached source/frame references are cleared when the source is removed.

What the extension processes on supported pages

The extension runs on chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com. It uses the page layout to position the wallpaper, follow the sidebar, and attach the controls you see.

Reading comfort visits message text nodes, checks whether they are empty, and measures their rendered rectangles to put blur behind the conversation. This happens locally. It does not save, summarize or transmit your conversation text or prompts.

When you open the toolbar, it briefly uses the active supported tab’s URL to identify the provider. Only the provider ID is retained in the popup’s memory. URLs, tab titles and browsing history are not stored or sent to the maintainer.

A displayed image is visible to the website

To show your wallpaper, the extension writes its displayed image URL into the supported page. Scripts on that page can read the displayed image from the shared DOM. Local extension storage does not make a displayed wallpaper secret from that website. Avoid sensitive images.

The extension does not read authentication cookies or send your image to an AI service. The supported websites continue to operate under their own privacy policies; LLM Wallpapers does not control them.

How data is used

Local data is used only to provide the wallpaper and visual controls you choose. The extension does not sell data, use it for profiling or advertisements, or send it to an unrelated service. It does not provide AI responses or call an external model.

Change, pause or delete

You can change effects and source choices in settings. Pausing hides the wallpaper without deleting its saved image. Switching to a bundled preset also keeps your upload so you can switch back.

Remove wallpaper clears the saved custom image, filename and preset selection. Other preferences remain. Uninstalling the extension normally removes its local extension storage through the browser. Browser/profile backups and operating-system storage are managed outside this extension.

There is no fixed expiry: your local choices remain until you change them, remove the source or uninstall. There is no maintainer-held copy of your image or settings to request from a server.

This launch page

This page includes no analytics scripts, cookies, tracking pixels, upload forms or remote fonts. Its demo uses original bundled artwork and synthetic conversation text. Visiting a hosted copy will still make normal requests for pages, assets and downloads; hosting-provider request logs depend on the final host. That hosting policy must be confirmed before this draft is published.

GitHub and the supported-provider links open external services only when you choose them. A support report you submit to GitHub is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not include private chats, full conversation URLs, settings exports, session state, passwords or sensitive images.

Contact and changes

The project is maintained by s-arbu on GitHub. Use the issue tracker for questions using a non-sensitive example.

This policy should be updated when the implementation or hosting changes. New collection, telemetry or cloud features would require a separate product/privacy decision; none are included in this candidate.
