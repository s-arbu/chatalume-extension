# v0.2.0 beta — release notes draft

Unpublished. Verify the final artifact and authenticated provider checks before release.

- Pause or resume a wallpaper directly from the right edge of ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. The action affects only the current provider and stays available while paused.
- Open the adjacent quick-controls panel to reset this site's effects or open full settings. Keyboard navigation, Escape dismissal, explicit on/off state, and a compact narrow-window layout are included.
- The rectangular sliding switch follows the chat's light/dark theme. General settings can hide the entire dock without pausing your wallpaper; keyboard and reduced-motion updates are instant.
- Reading comfort now offers Off / Enabled. Feathered blur follows actual message text while wallpaper gutters stay clear. New-chat screens and composers are untreated. Opaque Solid mode was removed; existing Solid preferences migrate to local blur. First installs default enabled; provider choices and reset-preserved preferences remain supported.
- The full settings tab supports independent provider effects, with a global pause that takes precedence.
- Choose original offline Mist, Dusk, and Paper presets, or retain your own upload while switching between them.
- Adjust blur, opacity, light/dark blend, fit, and position. Reset effects preserves your image; remove wallpaper clears saved sources.
- Settings saves survive closing the toolbar popup, and failed uploads preserve the previous wallpaper.

Images and settings remain local. Displayed wallpaper images are accessible to scripts on the supported website. No accounts, image-upload service, or telemetry are added.

Known limits: local fixture tests do not establish contrast on current signed-in provider layouts. Gemini's reported account-visibility issue was not reproduced in the current Brave layout and remains under investigation. Right-side provider panels and other extensions can occupy the quick control's preferred space; real-layout acceptance testing remains required.

Install the extracted ZIP through Chromium's Load unpacked beta workflow. Existing unpacked installations need a manual update/reload. When moving to a different extension identity or store installation, settings do not automatically transfer; preserve the original installation until a migration route is available.
